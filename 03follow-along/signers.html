Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence were:

Thomas Jefferson
Wrote most of the Declaration and later became the 3rd U.S. president.
John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress; famous for his unusually large signature.
Benjamin Franklin
Famous scientist, inventor, diplomat, and later the first U.S. Postmaster General.
John Adams
Major advocate for independence and later the 2nd U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
Important revolutionary leader and cousin of John Adams.
John Witherspoon
Minister and president of what became Princeton University; the only active college president to sign.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
One of the wealthiest men in the colonies and the last surviving signer.

Fun fact: John Hancock's famous signature wasn't actually meant to be funny or rebellious. As president of the Continental Congress, he signed first and prominently, which is why his name became synonymous with signing your name.
