Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship refers to the responsible and ethical use of technology and the internet. It encompasses understanding how to navigate the digital world safely, respectfully, and effectively.

What is digital law?

Digital law refers to the legal framework that governs the use of digital technology and the internet. It includes laws and regulations related to data privacy, cybersecurity, intellectual property, and online behavior.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is a set of rules and guidelines that define how technology and the internet should be used within an organization or institution. It outlines the acceptable and unacceptable uses of technology, as well as the consequences for violating the policy.

What is plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's work, ideas, or words without giving them proper credit. It is considered a form of academic dishonesty and can have serious consequences.

what is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty refers to any act of cheating or deception in an academic setting. This can include plagiarism, cheating on exams, or other forms of academic misconduct.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

The rules for AI generated content vary by organization and context, but generally include guidelines on how to properly attribute AI-generated content, ensure it is used ethically, and maintain transparency about its use.
