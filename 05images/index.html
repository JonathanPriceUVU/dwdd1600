Welcome to the Character Generator!

This tool will help you generate a playable character for a role-playing game. You can choose your character's race, class, background, and more. Once you've created your character, you can level up and customize them further. Let's get started!

Why use this page at all?

This site is primarily designed to quickly generate characters that are fleshed out enough to be used in game. Wether thats by the DM or a player that simply doesnt know where to start. This site walks throguh the proccess of generating ability scores, picking a race, class, and background; in that order.
It then provides further customization by providing optional feats, gear and spells.

the site will draw on the reference materials which will be listed spererately, for causual persual and reference purposes.
and the content will be sourced by a collection of core adventure books.

Let's get started!

First we will generate your character's ability scores. This will be done by rolling 3d6 for each of the six ability scores.

  1. Strength
  2. Dexterity
  3. Constitution
  4. Intelligence
  5. Wisdom
  6. Charisma

The next thing we need to do is decide on playable race. Each of which has unique abilities and traits.

•Dragonborn
Dragonborn are proud and honorable, with a strong sense of duty and a natural affinity for dragons.
  • Draconic Ancestry
  • Breath Weapon
  • Damage Resistance
•Dwarf
Dwarves are sturdy and resilient, with a strong connection to the earth and a talent for craftsmanship.
  • Darkvision
  • Dwarven Resilience
  • Stonecunning
•Elf
Elves are graceful and intelligent, with keen senses and a natural affinity for magic.
  • Darkvision
  • Fey Ancestry
  • Trance
•Gnome
Gnomes are curious and inventive, with a love of learning and a talent for illusion magic.
  • Darkvision
  • Gnome Cunning
  • Artificer's Lore
•Halfling
Halflings are nimble and lucky, with a cheerful disposition and a knack for stealth.
  • Lucky
  • Brave
  • Nimbleness
•Half-Elf
Half-elves are charismatic and versatile, with a blend of human and elven traits.
  • Darkvision
  • Fey Ancestry
  • Skill Versatility
•Half-Orc
Half-orcs are strong and intimidating, with a fierce determination and a natural talent for combat.
  • Darkvision
  • Relentless Endurance
  • Savage Attacks
•Human
Humans are versatile and adaptable, with a wide range of abilities and skills.
  • Extra language
  • +1 to all ability scores OR a feat
•Tiefling
Tieflings are cunning and resourceful, with a natural affinity for magic and a mysterious heritage.
  • Darkvision
  • Hellish Resistance
  • Infernal Legacy
